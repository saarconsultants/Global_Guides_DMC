## Scope

Whole-platform redesign, Operate mode. Audience: Indian B2B travel agents quoting fast; secondary admin staff. Task: quote, send, track, book. Proof: live supplier inventory and the agent's own brand. Constraints: keep every route, function, copy fact and supplier truth; no image generation tools (Higgsfield, OpenArt) this session; photography supplied by the owner.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every trip object is a boarding pass: a main panel with a strict label grid and a tear-off stub that carries the price and the one action. It refuses the portal's card-and-table sprawl.

OWN-WORLD: White card stock on cool grey ground; ink navy; crimson as carrier stripe and primary action; amber for the live cell. Archivo for UI, B612 Mono for codes, PNRs, prices and dates. Dashed perforation with notches; caps letterspaced labels over bold values; status as fixed-width caps chips.

STORY: The agent sees trips as passes ready to hand over, finds the next sale on one screen, and quotes it in the same grammar.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim white top bar. Photo hero, headline left, four service pills. The pass-shaped search bar (FROM · TO · DEPART · TRAVELLERS · crimson SEARCH stub) overlapping the hero's bottom edge. Below: three package passes and the desk list.

FORM: Boarding Pass, #1 of the safer grounded list; seed 38b725c1, re-roll 1, safer register; decision mock scratchpad/mocks/1-Boarding-Pass.png.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
