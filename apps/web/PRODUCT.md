## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: Indian B2B travel agents (sub-agents of Global Guides DMC) quoting a customer's trip, usually fast, while the customer waits on WhatsApp or a call. Desk browser most of the day; phone when out. Job: turn "Paris and Amsterdam, 7 nights, 2 adults" into a priced, bookable, branded proposal in minutes, send it, get it accepted, book it, and track money owed and earned.

Secondary: Global Guides staff (admin role) approving agencies, curating suggested itinerary templates, watching inventory health, commissions and revenue.

Tertiary: the agent's end customer, who sees only the agent's brand on a share page (`/p/[token]`), a PDF proposal, vouchers, and a widget embedded on the agent's own site.

Owner (Bipin) is a UI/UX designer and AI operator, not a coder. Investors will be shown the product. [confirmed]

## Product Purpose

White-label B2B travel platform: agents search live wholesale inventory (flights via Tripjack, hotels/activities/transfers via Hotelbeds), build itineraries manually or with the AI Trip Architect, send branded proposals, customers accept online, agents manage leads, bookings, team, wallet and statements. Success = an agent quotes and closes more trips per day than on a competitor portal, and the customer experiences the agent as a polished brand.

## Positioning

AI Trip Architect grounded in live bookable inventory: the AI routes cities and selects from numbered menus of real supplier options; nothing is invented, every line is bookable and priced. Competitor portals (Tripjack, TBO) are search-and-book utilities; this adds the whole quote-to-acceptance loop under the agent's own brand. [confirmed by owner]

## Operating Context

- Agents benchmark against Tripjack-style portals: hero band + unified search bar, dense results with fare/rate cards. [confirmed: owner supplied Tripjack screenshots as the reference for "modern"]
- Daily loop: dashboard (storefront: promotions, suggested packages, destinations, quick searches) → New trip (intake form or AI) → Customize (day-by-day builder with hotels, transfers, activities, flights, price summary) → Save & send proposal (PDF + share link) → Leads/Proposals tracking → Bookings → Statement/wallet.
- Currency shown in INR by default with a display-currency switch; prices are wholesale + agent markup.
- Owner supplies photography generated to prompts (ChatGPT Image 2.0): banners 16:9, destination tiles 4:5, hero backgrounds, login. Assets live in `public/promos/` (16 files). More can be requested via prompt. [confirmed]

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 15 App Router, Tailwind, Prisma/Postgres, iron-session; react-pdf proposals; OpenRouter LLM (server-side only).
- Multi-tenant by agencyId; share pages are token-only; supplier keys are server-side (Tripjack via a fixed-IP proxy). Live booking commit is deliberately not wired yet.
- Tripjack full-API access is imminent; hotels/transfers/sightseeing may move to Tripjack. [confirmed, pending]
- Terminology: Lead, Proposal (statuses DRAFT/SENT/VIEWED/ACCEPTED/BOOKED), Booking, Itinerary, Template ("Suggested"), Wallet, Statement, Agency, Team member, Markup, Board (Room Only / Breakfast Included…), Transfer.
- Routes: dashboard, itinerary/new, itinerary/[id]/customize, flights, hotels, hotels/[code], activities, holidays (fit/group/adhoc), suggested, leads, leads/[id], proposals, bookings, marketing, settings (+team, sales), statement, team, p/[token], widget/[slug], login, signup, invite/[token], admin/* (agencies, templates, inventory, commissions, revenue, performance, bug-reports).
- Images must never be generated by Higgsfield or OpenArt tools. [confirmed]

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Global Guides DMC. Logo: white wordmark with globe "G" on the maroon header (asset in app). Brand colours: deep crimson/maroon primary with an amber/gold accent; brand colours must "pop", not sit muted. [confirmed]

- Owner's stated bar: must not read as AI-generated or templated; "modern, outstanding, engaging"; agents are customers so imagery is consumer-grade; every page must be at the same quality, not only the home page. [confirmed]

## Product Principles

1. Speed to a sendable quote is the product; every screen shortens it.
2. Nothing invented: every hotel, fare, activity and price on screen is a real supplier line.
3. The agent's brand faces the customer; Global Guides' brand faces the agent.
4. Consumer-grade polish for a B2B audience; the tool should feel like the best travel apps agents already use on their phones.
5. One quality bar across all routes, including admin and settings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable forms and dialogs; contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for text; dates and money formatted for India (DD/MM/YYYY, ₹ with lakh grouping). [inferred from existing code, not separately confirmed]
